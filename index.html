Short Bio

I received the B.Eng. degree and the M.Eng. degree from Harbin Engineering University (HEU), China. I am currently pursuing the Ph.D. degree with the School of Information and Communications Engineering, Xi'an Jiaotong University (XJTU), China, advised by Prof. Lei Lei and Prof. Ang Li.

My research interests include constructive interference exploitation, symbol-level and block-level precoding, integrated sensing and communications (ISAC), signal processing, and MIMO wireless communications.
